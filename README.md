# PashuTrust
 About PashuTrust

PashuTrust is a digital platform designed to improve **trust, transparency, traceability, and accountability in the poultry supply chain**.

The platform connects:

- 👨‍🌾 Farmers
- 🧑‍⚕️ Inspectors
- 🛒 Buyers
- 🏛️ Authorities

Farmers can register their farms and maintain digital records of:

- Veterinary certificates
- Vaccination records
- Quarantine information
- Illness reports
- Animal/batch information

Inspectors can review these records and perform physical farm verification before approving a farm or animal batch.

Once approved, PashuTrust generates a **digital certification and QR code** that allows buyers to independently verify the authenticity and health-related certification of the poultry they purchase.

The platform also introduces a **reporting, rating, risk detection, and accountability mechanism** to discourage malpractice and improve trust across the ecosystem.

---

# 🚨 Problem Statement
The poultry sector faces several challenges related to:

- Fragmented health records
- Paper-based documentation
- Difficulty verifying farm information
- Limited visibility into vaccination and quarantine compliance
- Lack of transparent certification
- Difficulty identifying fraudulent or outdated records
- Limited buyer visibility into animal health information
- Lack of accountability for malpractice
- Difficulty tracking the complete verification history of animals

These issues can reduce trust between farmers, inspectors, buyers, and authorities.
